Chunking Strategies & Trade-offs

EX.03/10
Fixed-Size
Every N characters. Fast, but can cut mid-word or mid-sentence.
RedactedClick to open
Fixed + Overlap
Overlapping windows. Keeps boundary context, adds some redundancy.
RedactedClick to open
Sentence-Based
Groups whole sentences. Never splits mid-sentence, but variable size.
RedactedClick to open
Recursive
Paragraph → sentence → word fallback. The most common default.
RedactedClick to open
Token-Based
Splits by token count — matches the model's context limits precisely.
RedactedClick to open
Rule of thumb — start with recursive chunking plus a small overlap, then tune chunk size to your embedding model's sweet spot.

Embeddings: Turning Text into Meaning

Close together = similar meaning, even with zero shared words. Far apart = unrelated.

Similarity score: 0.73 — paraphrased, zero shared keywords, same meaning.

"Growth plan cost?" vs "encryption algorithm at rest?"
Similarity score: −0.01 — unrelated topics, correctly pushed apart.

RRF — Reciprocal Rank Fusion
score = Σ 1/(k+rank) — blends both rankings by position, robust even though BM25 and cosine scores live on different scales.
RedactedClick to open
One Retriever, Both Styles
